FULL SWR-STYLE ANNOUNCEMENTS
  1. Set Station to Kingston (KNG) or Norbiton (NBT).
  2. Under announcement options, enable Announce via points so the announcement can include the calling-at stations.
  3. Keep Next train, Approaching train, Standing train and Delays and cancellations enabled.
  4. Choose Use Celia Drummond on all platforms. Leave Use old TOC names off to prefer the current operator name, South Western Railway (SWR).
  5. Press Start live trains to enable audio.
Expected style: “The [time] South Western Railway service to [destination], calling at [stations]…” The exact wording and calling points depend on the live feed and available announcement recordings.

Use the official Rail Announcements live player below for authentic Celia Drummond railway audio. In its settings, choose Kingston (KNG) or Norbiton (NBT), choose Celia Drummond on all platforms, then press Start live trains.
